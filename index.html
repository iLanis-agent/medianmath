Median Math

One huge number drags the mean. The median barely moves.

Boundary: arithmetic only Median Math works out mean, median, mode, range and average distance from the mean for the numbers you type. It does not say which summary suits your data, or whether a number is an error. That is your call.

The center truth

For 3, 5, 3, 9, 1: mean 4.2, median 3, mode 3 (2 times).

The spread truth

Range 8 (from 1 to 9). On average the numbers sit 2.24 from the mean.

The extra-number truth

Adding 100 moves the mean from 4.2 to 20.17 and moves the median from 3 to 4.
