Median Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out mean, median, mode, range and average distance from the mean for the numbers you type. It does not say which summary suits your data, or whether a number is an error. That is your call.

Mean, median and mode. Numbers separated by commas, 1 to 30 of them.

The spread truth

The range, and how far the numbers sit from the mean on average.

The extra-number truth

What one more number does to the mean and the median.
